About

(01)

Same skill, pointed in different directions.

I am a value creator; I dedicate my life towards building a co-existing community towards a brighter future, whether through words or through actions. Heriot-Watt Empowerment Program · Life motto

I started with writing — blog posts, social campaigns and branded video scripts for Ajinomoto Malaysia, Vono, Truedan and Great Eastern, plus freelance articles across B2B, F&B, SEO, government grants and entertainment.

After a Master's in International Business Management, I moved into operations: automating financial reports, reconciling data systems, writing reports for executives, and keeping them ahead of deadlines instead of chasing them — as an Operations Analyst, an Account Strategist and a Personal Assistant.

Both turned out to be the same skill. Take the mess, make it clear enough that someone else can use it to make a decision.
