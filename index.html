01 / About

Structure first. Then the story that makes people act on it.

I started in operations — automating financial reports, reconciling data across systems, and keeping executives ahead of deadlines instead of chasing them. Somewhere along the way I picked up a second habit: writing the words that make people act. Blog posts for Ajinomoto's Food Biz Partner. Social campaigns for Vono Malaysia, Truedan, and aminoVITAL. Branded video scripts for Great Eastern's insurance agents, written across English, Mandarin, and Malay.

The two turned out to be the same skill, pointed in different directions: take something messy — a spreadsheet, a scattered thread of updates, a blank page — and turn it into something clear enough that someone else can use it immediately.

I hold a Master's in International Business Management from Heriot-Watt University Malaysia, and I'm based in Kuala Lumpur — open to remote and hybrid roles.
