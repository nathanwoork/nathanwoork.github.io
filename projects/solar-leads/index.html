Solar Leads Malaysia

A lead-research tool for solar installers: it finds business prospects from public map data, checks every contact against an official source, and exports a clean list a sales team can trust.

The problem

Lead lists are easy to make and hard to trust

Solar installers need commercial and industrial prospects, but bought or scraped lead lists are full of duplicates, outdated numbers and guesses dressed up as facts ("high electricity bill", "interested in solar"). Sales teams waste calls, and some lists cross the line on privacy.

I wanted a list where every field says where it came from and how sure it is.

How it works

A five-step pipeline with a person in the loop

Each scan picks a state and a prospect type. Leads then move through the same five steps, and nothing reaches the export until it has passed the checks.

  1. DiscoverPulls business and property records from OpenStreetMap.
  2. De-duplicateMatches stable record IDs, so repeat scans only bring new leads.
  3. VerifyConfirms address and phone on the official business site. Checks expire after 90 days.
  4. QualifyA reviewer records the call outcome, real bill and usable roof area.
  5. ExportFormatted Excel workbook with filters, or a CRM-ready CSV.

Data rules

Estimates are labelled as estimates

Every lead carries more than 30 fields. The rules I set for them keep the list honest:

  • Provenance: every phone number keeps the exact page it came from.
  • Confidence: electricity use, bill and supply level are labelled low- or medium-confidence until the owner confirms the real numbers.
  • No false negatives: "No solar found on the map" is recorded as unknown, never as "no solar".
  • Interest needs evidence: a lead is only marked "Interested" with a note of what the contact actually said.
  • Privacy: homes never have contact details pulled from maps; opt-outs are removed from every export.
